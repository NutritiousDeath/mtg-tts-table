MTG COMMANDER TABLE (TTS) - DECK TEST: JARED CARTHALION (THE LIEGES)
Four-player game
Version being tested: 1.69
After loading, chat should say:  MTG > Scripts loaded: version 1.69 (Jared Carthalion deck audit: color Lieges, all-color Kavus, Leyline, Oath of Teferi)

SETUP
- Four players, one per seat. One player is the JARED PLAYER; the other three are OPPONENTS (any decks).
- The tester for each line is named on the line: "Jared player", "any opponent" and so on.
- Each line is:  DO this  ->  EXPECT that.  Mark each line OK or WRONG.
- To test a card that hasn't come up naturally, the Jared player pulls it from the library
  (right-click library > Search) or drags it out.
- For anything WRONG: copy the chat lines from just before and after it into NOTES at the bottom.
- Numbers on cards: the big white number is power/toughness NOW (Lieges included). The small line under it
  says where the change comes from ("static +2/+2" = Lieges and other anthems).

BEFORE STARTING
[ ] 0a. Jared player imports the deck.  EXPECT: 100 cards, Jared Carthalion in the command zone, no warnings.
[ ] 0b. Check the import chat for MISS lines (newest printings: Lorwyn Eclipsed "ecl"/"ecc", Edge of Eternities
        "eoc", Final Fantasy "fic", Marvel "msc"). Copy any MISS lines into NOTES.
[ ] 0c. All four players import, start the game, mulligan and keep.

PART A - JARED CARTHALION (planeswalker commander)
=====================================================================
[ ] A1. Jared player casts Jared from the command zone.
        EXPECT: Jared lands with 5 loyalty and three buttons on the card (+1, -3, -6).
[ ] A2. Jared player clicks +1.
        EXPECT: loyalty 6, the ability goes on the stack. When it resolves: a 3/3 KAVU token with TRAMPLE.
        Hover the Kavu: its data says it is all colors (W U B R G).
[ ] A3. Jared player clicks +1 again the same turn.
        EXPECT: "already used a loyalty ability this turn" (no Oath of Teferi yet).
[ ] A4. Next turn, with a creature on the battlefield, Jared player clicks -3.
        EXPECT: COUNTERS buttons on creatures; up to two clicks; each creature gets a +1/+1 counter for each of its
        colors (a Kavu gets 5, a two-color Liege gets 2, a colorless artifact creature gets none). DONE stops early.
[ ] A5. With a multicolored card in the graveyard, Jared player uses -6.
        EXPECT: the graveyard search opens showing ONLY multicolored cards. Pick one: it goes to the hand.
[ ] A6. Same as A5 but return Transguild Courier (all colors).
        EXPECT: also "draws a card and creates two Treasures".
[ ] A7. Jared dies / is removed.  EXPECT: the offer to send him to the command zone.

PART B - THE LIEGES (the heart of the deck)
=====================================================================
Each Liege gives OTHER creatures of its two colors +1/+1 each. A creature of BOTH colors gets +2/+2 from it.
[ ] B1. Jared player has a Kavu (3/3) and casts Deathbringer Liege (white/black).
        EXPECT: Kavu shows 5/5 (+2/+2: it is white AND black). Deathbringer itself stays 3/4.
[ ] B2. Add Boartusk Liege (red/green).
        EXPECT: Kavu 7/7. Boartusk 3/4 (Deathbringer doesn't touch red/green). Deathbringer 3/4.
[ ] B3. Add a third Liege that shares a color with another (e.g. Balefire Liege, red/white).
        EXPECT: Balefire gets +1/+1 from Deathbringer (white) and +1/+1 from Boartusk (red) = 4/6.
        Deathbringer gets +1/+1 from Balefire (white). Boartusk gets +1/+1 from Balefire (red).
[ ] B4. Each of the ten Liege lines works (check off as they come up):
        [ ] Ashenmoor (B/R)   [ ] Balefire (R/W)     [ ] Boartusk (R/G)   [ ] Creakwood (B/G)
        [ ] Deathbringer (W/B) [ ] Glen Elendra (U/B) [ ] Mindwrack (U/R)  [ ] Murkfiend (G/U)
        [ ] Thistledown (W/U) [ ] Wilt-Leaf (G/W)
        EXPECT for each: creatures of its colors that the Jared player controls get +1/+1 per matching color;
        OPPONENTS' creatures are not affected.
[ ] B5. A Liege leaves the battlefield.  EXPECT: the numbers drop back within about a second.
[ ] B6. With all 10 Lieges out, a Kavu shows 23/23.

LIEGE ABILITIES
[ ] B7. Balefire Liege out; Jared player casts a RED spell.
        EXPECT: a trigger: 3 damage to a target player (pick one). A WHITE spell: the Jared player gains 3 life.
        A red-AND-white spell (e.g. Boros Charm, Figure of Destiny): both triggers.
[ ] B8. Deathbringer Liege out; Jared player casts a WHITE spell.
        EXPECT: "tap a creature?" with TAP buttons on creatures (or SKIP). The clicked creature turns sideways.
[ ] B9. Deathbringer out; Jared player casts a BLACK spell.
        EXPECT: may destroy a TAPPED creature (only tapped creatures get the button).
        Casting a white-AND-black spell (e.g. another hybrid W/B card, Anguished Unmaking): tap first, then
        destroy the same creature (the classic combo).
[ ] B10. Creakwood Liege out: at the Jared player's upkeep.
         EXPECT: "may create a 1/1 black and green Worm" (YES/NO). The Worm gets the Liege bonuses.
[ ] B11. Murkfiend Liege out. During an OPPONENT's untap step:
         EXPECT: the Jared player's tapped GREEN and/or BLUE creatures untap too (chat: "untaps N during
         <SEAT>'s untap step (Murkfiend Liege)"). Non-green, non-blue creatures stay tapped.
[ ] B12. Any opponent targets Ashenmoor Liege with a spell or ability.
         EXPECT: that opponent loses 4 life.
[ ] B13. Mindwrack Liege: activate {U/R}{U/R}{U/R}{U/R}.
         EXPECT: chat says to put a blue or red creature from the hand onto the battlefield BY HAND (drag it).
[ ] B14. Thistledown Liege has flash (cast it in an opponent's turn).
[ ] B15. Wilt-Leaf Liege discarded by an OPPONENT's effect: put it onto the battlefield BY HAND (not automated).
