Forge effects (ApiType steps) tools/forge_effects.py maps (Forge 64569999, 2026-10-16).
Regenerate with tools/forge_triggers.py (it writes both data files). Mana abilities are left to mana.lua.

How the table uses them (effects.lua Effects.parseBest): each step becomes one plain sentence the
rules-text reader already handles, or one of the table's own actions where it has no words (damage to
a creature, exile itself, pay-or-sacrifice, "that creature", flicker, delayed end-step actions,
animate, gain control, regenerate). Forge's reading is used only when the
card's text reads to nothing or to fewer actions; on a tie, or when the text reading uses one of the
table's hand-written handlers (The One Ring, Castle Locthwain...), the text's reading stands.
Paragraphs with "if", "unless" or "instead" are always read from the text.
Counts (X = creatures you control, its power, counters on it, cards in hand / graveyard, life,
domain) are worked out when the ability resolves and said in chat. A count that a step before it
would change, or the power / counters of a card that has just left the battlefield, is left to the text.
