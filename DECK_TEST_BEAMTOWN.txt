MTG COMMANDER TABLE (TTS) - DECK TEST: THE BEAMTOWN BULLIES
Version being tested: 1.65
After loading, chat should say:  MTG > Scripts loaded: version 1.65 (Beamtown Bullies deck audit)

HOW TO USE THIS
- Two seats are enough: type  !solo on  and play Bullies at one seat and a "victim" deck at another.
- Import the deck, start the game, then pull the cards you're testing into your hand
  (right-click library > Search, or drag them out).
- One line at a time:  DO this  ->  EXPECT that.  Mark each line OK or WRONG.
- When something is WRONG, copy the chat lines from just before and after it into NOTES at the bottom.
- The Bullies ability only works on an OPPONENT'S turn. For those tests, pass the turn to the other seat first
  (in solo you can act for both).

PART B - THE "GIFTS" (what Bullies hands to opponents)
=====================================================================
Do each of these twice: once cast by yourself (it should hurt YOU), once given away with Bullies
(it should hurt THE OPPONENT). Watch that it's always the right player.

BRONZE BOMBSHELL
[ ] B1. DO: give Bronze Bombshell to an opponent with Bullies.
        EXPECT: a trigger for the opponent; when it resolves they sacrifice it and lose 7 life.
        Bombshell goes to YOUR graveyard (you own it), so you can give it again.
[ ] B2. DO: cast Bombshell yourself.  EXPECT: nothing happens (you own it and control it).

THORNBITE STAFF COMBO (Bullies + Thornbite Staff + Bombshell)
[ ] B3. DO: equip Thornbite Staff to Bullies.
        EXPECT: Bullies' right-click menu gains "Activate: {2}, {T}" (deal 1 damage). The Staff itself has NO
        odd "Activate: Equipped creature has..." line any more.
[ ] B4. DO: on an opponent's turn, Bullies gives them Bombshell; let the Bombshell trigger resolve.
        EXPECT: Bombshell dies -> a "whenever a creature dies, untap this creature" trigger for Bullies
        (chat: "[ability given by Thornbite Staff]") -> Bullies untaps. You can activate Bullies again and repeat.
        Each loop is one click per step: the table must NOT run it by itself.
[ ] B5. DO: also try Sting (untaps the equipped creature at the start of EACH combat, opponents' too)
        and Patriar's Seal / Formidable Speaker (untap target).  EXPECT: each untaps Bullies.

WORLDGORGER DRAGON
[ ] B6. DO: cast Worldgorger Dragon yourself.
        EXPECT: its trigger exiles ALL your other permanents (lands too). Chat says they come back when it leaves.
[ ] B7. DO: remove Worldgorger (Terminate it, or drag it to the graveyard).
        EXPECT: every exiled permanent comes back to its owner's battlefield (lands to the lands row), and their
        "enters" triggers happen again.
[ ] B8. DO: give Worldgorger to an opponent with Bullies, then go to the end step.
        EXPECT: THEIR other permanents are exiled; at the end step Worldgorger is exiled (to YOUR exile) and their
        permanents come back.
        WATCH: TTS lag with a big board. If cards stay in the exile pile, send chat lines.

[ ] B9. DO: Hellcarver (given or yours) deals combat damage to a player.

INVERTER OF TRUTH

LEVELER

SIRE OF INSANITY

         EXPECT: trigger; every player discards their whole hand.

SOULGORGER ORGG

[ ] B18. DO: end step (Orgg is exiled).  EXPECT: they gain back exactly what they lost.

BANE OF PROGRESS / HAYWIRE MITE / DRAGON MAGE / MAGUS OF THE WHEEL
