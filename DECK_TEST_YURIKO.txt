MTG COMMANDER TABLE (TTS) - DECK TEST: YURIKO, THE TIGER'S SHADOW (NINJAS)
Version being tested: 1.66
After loading, chat should say:  MTG > Scripts loaded: version 1.66 (Yuriko deck audit: ninjutsu, sneak, empty-library wins)

HOW TO USE THIS
- Two seats are enough: type  !solo on  and play Yuriko at one seat against any deck at another.
- Import the deck, start the game, then pull the cards you're testing into your hand
  (right-click library > Search, or drag them out).
- One line at a time:  DO this  ->  EXPECT that.  Mark each line OK or WRONG.
- When something is WRONG, copy the chat lines from just before and after it into NOTES at the bottom.
- Ninjutsu needs a real combat: attack with something small, let the other seat block (or not), and get to the
  BLOCK step with BLOCKS DONE clicked.

        EXPECT: RETURN buttons only on your unblocked attackers. Click one: it goes back to your hand, the mana is

        attacking <PLAYER>" item goes on the stack.

        the red glow, attacking the SAME player the returned creature attacked.

[ ] A7. DO: go to damage.  EXPECT: the ninja is in the COMBAT DAMAGE panel and its "deals combat damage" trigger
        happens (Ninja of the Deep Hours: you may draw).
[ ] A8. DO: an opponent's creature taps for ninjutsu?  EXPECT: only the ATTACKING player can use it.
