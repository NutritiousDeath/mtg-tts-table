== v1.48: TOKEN PILES (OFF by default; try in !solo on first)

[ ] Treasure / Clue / Food and any token with an ability (not just keywords) are NEVER piled.
[ ] Pick a pile up: you take ONE token, the rest stay behind as a pile (badge drops by one).

[ ] Put a +1/+1 counter on one token (right-click): it leaves the pile on its own, the counter token is single.
[ ] Mass effects hit the whole pile: Avenger landfall / Kalonian Hydra / proliferate put the counter on every token of the pile (the pile shows the counter and keeps its xN).
[ ] Attach Equipment to a pile / click ATTACK? / click BLOCK? / click a sacrifice or target button: one token comes off, the rest stay.
[ ] "for each creature you control" and metalcraft count every token in a pile.
[ ] Sweeper: drag a whole pile to the graveyard (or right-click > Pile: all die): every token dies (one dies event each: Blood Artist style drains N times).
[ ] Second Harvest copies a pile as a pile of the same size.
[ ] New plain tokens merge into a matching pile after ~2s and at cleanup/upkeep. Tapped, counter-ed, equipped or summoning-sick tokens stay separate from the others.
