[ ] Academy Manufactor on battlefield, make a Treasure / Food / Clue: you get one of each.
[ ] Karn the Great Creator -2 or Urza ETB: Construct token appears and shows +1/+1 per artifact you control (add an artifact, check the P/T).
[ ] Intangible Virtue: creature tokens get +1/+1.
[ ] Hangarback Walker: put 3 +1/+1 counters on it, send to graveyard: you get 3 Thopters (if the Thopter doesn't appear, use the TOKENS tile and tell me).
